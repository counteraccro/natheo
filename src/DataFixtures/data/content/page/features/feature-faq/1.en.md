## Questions sorted into categories

A FAQ is made of categories, and each category of questions. The general FAQ of this site has three of them, called "Installation", "Content" and "API and themes".

Questions and answers are entered in the three languages. Answers are written in Markdown, with the same editor as pages, so they can hold links, lists or code samples.

In the administration, the order of categories and questions is changed in a few clicks. A category or a question can be disabled to remove it from the site without deleting it, while you update an answer for example. The whole FAQ can also be hidden with the "FAQ visible on the site" setting.
