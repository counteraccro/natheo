## Eleven menu types

| Position | Types |
|---|---|
| Header | dropdown menu, mega menu from 1 to 4 columns, sidebar |
| Sides | expanded menu, accordion menu |
| Footer | columns, one row aligned right or centred |

A menu item leads to a page of the site or to an external address, in the same tab or a new one. Items can be nested and reordered by drag and drop.
