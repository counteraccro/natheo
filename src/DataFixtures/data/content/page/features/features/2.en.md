## Working together

Each piece of content is translated into [French, English and Spanish](/en/page/multilingual), with a different address in each language.

An account has the contributor, administrator or super administrator role. A contributor edits their own content. When page validation is on, their pages go through an administrator before being published.

[Permissions](/en/page/permissions-and-validation) also protect content used elsewhere. A FAQ shown in a page cannot be deleted, and only an administrator can delete another user's content.
