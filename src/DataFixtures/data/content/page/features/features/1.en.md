## Content

A page is made of blocks, arranged in one of the [eight layouts](/en/page/pages-and-layouts). A block holds Markdown text, a FAQ or the list of the latest pages of a category.

[Menus](/en/page/menus) go in the header, on the sides or in the footer, with eleven display types. A [FAQ](/en/page/built-in-faq) can have its own page or sit in the middle of another one.

Images and documents are sorted into folders in the [media library](/en/page/media-library). This is also where you pick the header image of each page.
