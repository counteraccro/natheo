## Page validation

The "Page validation" system option adds a review before publication. When it is on, a contributor no longer publishes directly. They submit their page, which becomes read-only. They can withdraw the submission as long as it has not been handled, to go back to editing the page.

Administrators get a notification, and pending pages appear in the "Pages to review" block of the dashboard. The administrator opens the page, fixes it if needed, then approves or rejects it. An approved page is published right away. A rejected page can be edited again, with the reason for the rejection shown at the top of the editor, and the contributor is notified in both cases.

On this demo site, a blog post is waiting for validation. Log in with an administrator account to see it on the dashboard.
