## Comment moderation

Comments are opened or closed for the whole site in the system options, then page by page. Each page also says whether a new comment is published right away or waits for approval. The "Require approval of new comments" option enforces approval everywhere, whatever the page setting.

A pending comment stays in its place on the site, with a message saying it will be published after approval. A moderated comment is replaced by a message for visitors. The moderator can write the reason for the moderation, which only team members see.

Moderation happens in the "Comments" screen of the administration, one by one or in bulk. A logged-in administrator can also approve or moderate a comment directly under the page, on the site.
