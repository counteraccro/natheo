## Roles

Each account has a role, which decides the administration menus it can access.

A contributor creates pages, FAQs, menus and tags. They can edit, disable or delete their own, but not those of others. An administrator can act on all content, whoever wrote it, and also manages users. The super administrator has access to the "System" section, which holds the site options, API tokens, the translation tool and updates. They can also log in as another account to check what that account sees.

Two safeguards come on top of these roles. Content used elsewhere cannot be deleted, such as a FAQ placed in a page. And the "Allow data deletion" option, once turned off, prevents any deletion, even for an administrator.
