## Writing a page

The text of a block is written in Markdown, in an editor with a toolbar and a preview. A page also has tags, a description and keywords for search engines, a header image picked from the media library and its own rules for comments.

A page that has never been published is updated on every save. Once it is live, it works differently. Your saves go into a draft, flagged by a banner at the top of the editor, and the published version stays on the site. You can preview the draft on the site, publish it to replace the live version, or discard it.

The "History" tab keeps the successive changes and lets you go back to a previous state. If the browser closes before you save, Nathéo offers to restore the work in progress when you reopen the page.
