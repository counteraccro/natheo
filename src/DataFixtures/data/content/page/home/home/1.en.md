## Welcome to the Nathéo demo site

Nathéo is an open-source CMS. The server is written with Symfony 8 and the administration with Vue 3 and TypeScript. It lets you build a site in French, English and Spanish, with pages split into blocks, menus, FAQs, a media library and comments.

The site you are reading was installed with Nathéo and filled with its demo data. Each page presents a part of the CMS by using it. The page about menus is displayed with a mega menu in the header, the documentation has its table of contents in a menu on the left, and the blog lists its posts with a listing block. Nothing was added by hand to the theme to get this result.

This data is loaded by the `natheo:install` command. After an installation, you get the same site on your machine, with the administration on `/en/admin/login` to see how each page is built.
