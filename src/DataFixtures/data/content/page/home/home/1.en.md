## A site that looks like you, without giving up code quality

Nathéo is an **open-source** content management system built with **Symfony 8** and **Vue 3**. It brings together what you expect from a modern CMS: pages made of blocks, menus of every shape, a FAQ, a media library and comments, all in **French, English and Spanish**.

This site is itself powered by Nathéo: every page you browse shows a feature of the CMS.
