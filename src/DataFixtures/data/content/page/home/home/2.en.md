### For editors

You create a page by picking one of the eight available layouts, then filling its blocks with Markdown text, a FAQ or a list of pages.

Once a page is live, your saves go into a draft. The published version stays on the site until you click "Publish", and you can preview the draft on the site before publishing it.

When the page validation option is on, a contributor submits their page and an administrator reviews it before it goes live. If the page is rejected, the administrator gives a reason that the contributor finds in the editor.

Comments from every page arrive in a single moderation screen. A logged-in administrator can also moderate them directly on the site.
