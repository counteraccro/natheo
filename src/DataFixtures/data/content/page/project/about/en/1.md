## The project

Nathéo was born from a simple wish: a CMS that does not force you to choose between ease of use and code quality. Developed as free software, it moves forward release after release, at the pace of its users' needs.

After a 1.0 that laid the foundation, 2.0 rebuilt it, and 2.1 paves the way for a Nathéo entirely driven by its API.
