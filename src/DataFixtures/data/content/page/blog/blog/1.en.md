## The Nathéo journal

This blog follows how Nathéo evolves. You will find the announcement of each release, with what it changes and what to know before upgrading, and posts about how the CMS is built, such as the support for two databases.

Posts are open to comments. A new comment waits for an administrator's approval before it appears under the post.
