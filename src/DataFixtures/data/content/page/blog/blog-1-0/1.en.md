Nathéo's first stable release came out on **7 October 2025**, after two betas.

It laid the project's foundation: managing pages, menus, FAQs and the media library, an administration interface, multilingual support and a modular architecture. It also moved from Webpack Encore to **Vite** and formatted the code with Prettier.

A year later, 2.0 rebuilt everything on these foundations, and 2.1 takes over the public site.
